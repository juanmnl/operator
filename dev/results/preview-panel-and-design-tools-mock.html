Preview beside the Console

The preview is one surface per session. It sits either in the main view or in the side panel, and moving it to one place takes it out of the other. The layout grid and redlines are overlays you switch on in either place. Full spec: dev/results/preview-panel-and-design-tools-design.md.

Try

  • Drag the panel's left edge. Under 880px the preview bar splits into an address row and a tools row.
  • Click Plan, then Preview: the panel remembers a width for each.
  • Click Preview in the top toolbar to move it to the main view.

Measure

  • Hover the pricing page to see an element's size and its distance to its container.
  • ⌥-click to set an anchor, then hover another element. Esc clears it.
  • Switch device to 375, 768 or 1280: the Auto grid moves between 4, 8 and 12 columns.

The white page inside the preview stands in for the lane's own app, so its colours are that app's. Everything around it uses Operator's Mission Control tokens and follows your light or dark setting. Shortcuts in this mock: ⌘' grid, ⌘⇧' redlines, ⌘E Interact ⇄ Annotate.
