**Operator 0.28.0: a crashed window comes back with its lanes, and a relaunch offers every lane you had open.**

- **A crashed window reloads by itself.** Before, when the window's renderer died, the window stayed black until you quit, and quitting ended every lane. Now the window reloads, and the lanes that kept running in the background reattach with their scrollback.
  - If the window crashes more than 3 times in a minute, it stops reloading and shows a page with a Reload button. Your lanes keep running.
  - Each crash is recorded in `~/.operator/logs/renderer-gone.log`.
  - Lane dispatches and replies sent while the window reloads are held and delivered once the lanes are back. Ones older than 5 minutes are shown to you instead of acted on.
  - Preview's Inspect and Redlines work again after a reload, and a crash reload does not bring Operator to the front.
- **A relaunch offers every lane, in every project.** Before, the relaunch kept only the lanes of the project you last had open and dropped the others. Now a card at the top of the overview reads "Resume N lanes from your last session", with a count per project. Its Resume button restarts exactly those lanes, oldest first, in the background. The card stays until the lanes are back or you dismiss it.
  - A lane is never started twice, even if you also resume it from the sidebar, Project Home or ⌘K while the card is working.
  - Lanes that ended on their own, that you closed or retired, or that finished and released their worktree are not offered. A finished lane's worktree is never rebuilt.
  - Lanes the card cannot resume, or that failed to start, are named on the card.
- **After a crash of the whole app, the lanes come back on their own.** Operator now keeps the list of running lanes in `~/.operator/last-run.json`. If the previous run did not quit normally, the next launch resumes those lanes in the background and names them in a notice. After a normal quit you get the card only; the "resume on launch" setting still decides that case.
  - A crash record older than 24 hours is offered on the card, not resumed.
  - If the app crashes again within 2 minutes of resuming, the lanes are offered on the card instead of resumed again.
- **Security fixes.**
  - Text written into a lane's terminal (dispatches, replies, Preview notes, dropped file names) is stripped of control characters, so it can no longer end Operator's paste early and run commands in the lane.
  - A dispatched task can no longer be read as an option of the lane's `claude` command.
  - A previewed page can no longer send a note to a lane by itself. Its note waits in a card that shows the full text, and only your click sends it.
- **Reports reach every idle coordinator.** Reports from lanes are announced to every idle coordinator, re-checked every 5 seconds, and old ones are summarised in one line instead of being dropped.

Updating from 0.27.1: the update ends your running lanes, as before. This first launch has no `last-run.json` yet, so the card is built from the window's own saved list.
