**Operator 0.27.1: a finished lane is replaced on its own, so coordinators stop asking you to close it.**

- **Coordinators no longer ask you to close a lane before continuing.** Since 0.27.0, new work goes to a fresh worktree. A lane that had finished and released its worktree still held its role, so the coordinator asked you to close it by hand. Now, when work is dispatched to that role:
  - If the finished lane is idle, Operator ends it and starts a fresh lane with the task.
  - If it is still busy, the dispatch is refused, and the coordinator gets one message when the lane is idle.
- **A lane is never ended while it has work.** Anything you or a coordinator send it after it releases its worktree keeps it running. Replies and `[Operator]` notices do not count as new work.
- **Replies never start or end a lane.** A reply goes to the running lane or is refused. Replies from lanes are no longer held for approval, and a reply from a session outside the project's lanes is refused.
- **Uncommitted work in a released worktree is kept** when its lane is ended.
- **A finished lane that closes itself for being idle is ended, not suspended,** so the next task starts on a fresh branch instead of the merged one.
