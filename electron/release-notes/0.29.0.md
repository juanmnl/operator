**Operator 0.29.0: messages from other lanes wait while you type, lanes and projects are easier to close, links open without trailing punctuation, and a source of renderer memory growth is fixed.**

- **Messages wait while you are typing.** A dispatch, reply or report sent to a lane used to be pasted into whatever you had typed in its composer, and sent with it.
  - It now waits until you send or clear your text. A pill at the top of the pane says how many messages are waiting, with a **Deliver now** button that adds the first one to your text and sends both.
  - Opening a `/` command, an `@` file, an emoji or a channel list counts as typing. Clearing the composer with Esc Esc ends the wait.
  - Waiting messages survive a window reload.
  - A lane with a waiting message or unsent text is not closed automatically, suspended or restarted.
- **A lane menu.** Right-click a lane in the rail, collapsed or expanded, for Close session, "Suspend — keep the conversation", Lane colour, and Restart when a newer Claude Code is installed. A lane you suspend by hand stays suspended until you open it.
- **Closing lanes and projects.**
  - On the expanded rail, a row's close button (■) also appears on keyboard focus, not only on hover.
  - Close project opens one dialog that lists every lane it will end, mid-turn lanes first. It is available from the rail and gallery ⋯ menus, a Close project button on the project home, and ⌘K.
  - After a close you see one toast. If a lane does not stop, the toast names it and offers Retry.
  - ⌘W on a lane that is mid-turn asks for a second press.
  - The Team card's ■ now closes suspended lanes too.
  - Forget project is disabled while the project has lanes open.
- **Terminal links.** A link followed by a comma, full stop or other sentence punctuation, a closing quote or bracket, or markdown `**`, opens without it. A link that wraps across rows opens whole. The underline on hover now covers the same text the click opens.
- **Renderer memory.** The main window kept old copies of each lane's message list on every lane update and every project change, and kept a full copy of the saved projects in memory. Both are fixed. In a two-hour test with live lanes, the retained copies stayed flat instead of growing. It is not yet confirmed that this was all of the growth of about 30 MB an hour that 0.28.4's notes mention.
